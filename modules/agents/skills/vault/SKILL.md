name: vault
description: Open a selected Obsidian vault, find or resume context, and help record reflections, ideas, todos, or project updates using that vault's own guidance. Use for conversational context dumps and work, personal, or server-vault requests.
---

1. Resolve the target vault from the owner's request, established context, or current directory. An explicit target takes precedence. Ask if the vault or remote access is unclear; never substitute another vault.
2. Read its `AGENTS.md` and applicable nested instructions, then open only the referenced guidance relevant to the request. If it has no `AGENTS.md`, consult its existing entry point or conventions and clarify missing guidance.
3. Follow that vault's workflow for listening, reflection, organization, and recording. Use existing destinations; ask when intent or destination is unclear and before introducing a new section or workflow. Keep vaults separate unless the owner requests otherwise.
4. Report what was saved and where, or what remains unsaved. Do not infer synchronization, backup, or publication from a note edit.
